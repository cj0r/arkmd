Settings > Autosave delay: choose 3 seconds, 10 seconds (default), 30 seconds or 1 minute after you stop typing; continuous typing is saved at least every 2 minutes, and leaving the window saves at once. Includes the matching file menu in the recent files pane and the earlier pane, delete, right-click and Android folder changes.

This is a beta build, published for testing: it can have bugs. To go back, turn off Settings > Updates > Include beta versions and install the latest release. Android cannot install an older version over a newer one without uninstalling ArkMD first.
